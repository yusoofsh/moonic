# Moonic Product Marketing Context

## Product Overview
Moonic is a VSCode theme extension that blends the best elements from Electronic Dawn, Catppuccin Noctis, and Maron Themes with opinionated design decisions.

## Target Audience
- VSCode developers who value carefully curated color schemes
- Developers tired of generic, one-size-fits-all themes
- Users who appreciate opinionated design choices
- Developers who spend long hours coding and need eye-comfortable colors

## Key Differentiators
1. **Carefully curated colors** — Every shade chosen for contrast, readability, and eye comfort
2. **Best-of-breed blend** — Combines proven color choices from beloved themes
3. **Opinionated design** — Strong decisions about what matters in a theme
4. **Multiple variants** — Light, Primary (dark), and Secondary (dark) options

## Brand Voice & Tone
- **Casual & developer-friendly** — Approachable, honest tone
- **Confident but not arrogant** — Clear point of view without being pushy
- **Minimal marketing speak** — Direct communication, no buzzwords
- **Authentic** — "This is what I use" rather than "revolutionary solution"

## Marketing Goals
- Stand out from the thousands of generic VSCode themes
- Attract developers who value quality over quantity
- Build credibility through transparency about influences
- Emphasize the curated, opinionated nature as a feature

## Key Messages
- "A theme that doesn't compromise"
- "Opinionated by design"
- "Colors that don't fight for attention"
- "Not trying to be everything to everyone"

## Proof Points
- Based on three popular, well-loved themes
- Active development with regular updates
- Three distinct variants for different preferences
- Focused on real-world use cases (debugging at 2am, reviewing PRs)
